Andre Executor privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026. Andre Malan operates this private integration for his own authorized accounts. Questions: andre@k-id.com.

Google access and use

The Gmail permission lets the integration read, search, compose, send and organize mail, including attachments, labels and drafts. It does not grant permanent deletion or Gmail settings changes. The Drive permission lets it read, create, edit, share and delete files and work with Docs, Sheets and Slides. Access is used to carry out Andre’s requested work through his chosen clients.

Storage and access

Authorization tokens are stored by the self-hosted Executor service in its persistent data volume. The service uses these tokens to call Google APIs. Connected clients select saved accounts and tools through scoped Executor connections. Tool results and execution records may remain in Executor, client histories, logs or backups under the settings of those systems.

Sharing

Requested Google data can be returned to Andre’s connected Claude or Codex client and processed by the associated model provider to complete the task. Those providers’ account settings and terms govern their handling of client conversations. Data is not sold or used by this integration for advertising. Sending mail or sharing files can disclose information to recipients selected by Andre.

Control and deletion

Andre can disconnect accounts in Executor and revoke Google access in his Google Account’s third-party connections settings. Revoking a grant prevents further authorized API access; it does not erase earlier results or backups. Stored records must be removed through the relevant service or client’s controls and backup retention process.

Limited use

Andre Executor’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
