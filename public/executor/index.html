Andre Executor

Andre Executor is Andre Malan’s private service integration. It connects his authorized Google accounts to a self-hosted Executor instance so his chosen Claude and Codex clients can work with Gmail and Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides.

Gmail access covers reading, searching, composing, sending and organizing mail. Drive access covers finding, reading, creating, editing, sharing and deleting files, including documents, spreadsheets and presentations. The connected account’s Google permissions apply to every action.

The integration is for Andre’s own use. It is not a publicly shared application, and this information page does not provide access to the Executor service.

Read the privacy policy for how Google data and authorization are handled. Contact: andre@k-id.com.
